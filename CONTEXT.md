# Guided Reflection

This context describes the language of a private reflection that turns a person's own answers into cautious, testable directions. It also covers purchasing and receiving the complete result without creating an account.

## Language

**Reflection**:
The complete guided journey through the fixed questions and optional clarifications.
_Avoid_: Assessment, test, quiz

**Reflection Session**:
One anonymous, resumable instance of a Reflection and its saved progress.
_Avoid_: Account, user profile

**Reflection Answer**:
A person's own response to a fixed question or Coach Prompt.
_Avoid_: AI answer, submission

**Coach Prompt**:
An optional AI-generated question that helps a person make one of their Reflection Answers more concrete or precise.
_Avoid_: Advice, recommendation, answer suggestion

**Compass**:
The cautious interpretation generated from a completed Reflection, with each important claim connected to supporting Reflection Answers.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis, personality profile, verdict

**Compass Preview**:
A useful but deliberately limited part of a Compass that can be viewed before purchase.
_Avoid_: Free result, teaser

**Full Compass**:
The complete Compass, including its directions, evidence, and action-planning material.
_Avoid_: Premium analysis, paid answer

**Report Purchase**:
A single payment that unlocks one Reflection Session's Full Compass and its delivery by email.
_Avoid_: Subscription, membership

**Full Compass Entitlement**:
The permission created by a successful Report Purchase to access one Full Compass.
_Avoid_: Plan, tier

**Report Recipient**:
The email address supplied during a Report Purchase to receive the Full Compass.
_Avoid_: User, account holder, subscriber
